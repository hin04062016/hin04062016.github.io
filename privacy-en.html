Privacy policy

繁體中文

This policy covers the ToilyGo iOS app and this support website. The operator is Yu Hin Lai. Contact: hinsstudio2023@gmail.com.

Location and maps

With your permission, the app reads location and heading while in use to find nearby places and provide walking directions. You may deny location access and select a city manually, or withdraw permission in iPhone settings. We do not run continuous background tracking or maintain a server-side journey history. Apple Maps processes map, search, network and relevant location information according to Apple's policies.

On-device information

Favorites, report drafts, language, appearance and search preferences are stored on your device. No account registration or account-based synchronization is required. System backups depend on your iOS settings.

Email reports and support

Email reporting passes the description you wrote, place information (including selected coordinates for a new place) and a report reference to your mail app for you to review and send. ToilyGo does not automatically send the report to support. Once you send it, we receive its content, sender email address and normal email metadata to reply, investigate and verify place information. Please omit sensitive or unnecessary personal information.

Reports are not published automatically. Verified facility facts may be included in later data updates; sender addresses and complete emails are not published. We retain correspondence as needed to handle the issue. You may request access, correction or deletion, subject to any legally required retention.

Google AdMob

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform to show banners in eligible home, favorites and report-history views. It requests non-personalized ads, disables publisher first-party ID and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. We do not attach precise GPS coordinates, search terms, favorites or report content to ad requests.

Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. Google's SDK may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostic and performance data for ad serving, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Consent choices are presented where applicable. You may revisit Advertising and privacy options in app settings when Google requires that option. Google's own policies describe its processing and retention.

Other services and this website

Place sources include public authorities, OpenStreetMap and venue operators. External websites apply their own policies when opened. This support website has no added advertising trackers or analytics scripts; its hosting provider may process connection logs and IP addresses for operations and security. Email, Apple, Google and hosting providers may process data outside your country.

Your controls and contact

You may remove favorites and report drafts, revoke location permission, or uninstall the app and its local data. Removing a local draft does not delete an email already sent. Send privacy requests to hinsstudio2023@gmail.com. We may reasonably verify your connection to the requested information. ToilyGo is a general travel and lifestyle utility, not designed specifically for children.

Changes

We update this page and its date when practices change and provide appropriate notice for material changes.
